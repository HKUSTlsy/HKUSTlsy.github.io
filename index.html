Complex systems.

04 — SELECTED RESEARCH PROJECTS

From measured signals to models

2021–2022

Bridge modal identification

Nonstationary wind · Central South University

Processed bridge monitoring data in MATLAB, extracted time-varying mean wind, and compared covariance-driven stochastic subspace identification (SSI-COV) with empirical wavelet transform and Hilbert transform (EWT-HT). Contributed to analysis and manuscript drafting and revision.

2022–2023

Adaptive wind-field simulation

Nonstationary wind fields · Central South University

Investigated adaptive interpolation-node selection and two-dimensional Hermite time–frequency interpolation in MATLAB. Contributed to the team’s methodology and data curation.

2019–2022

Full-scale bridge wind monitoring

Field data · Construction-stage buffeting

Worked on online acquisition and storage of wind and acceleration data, data cleaning, wind-characteristic calculation, and response analysis. The related work included the Pingtan Strait Road-Rail Bridge wind-resistance study.
